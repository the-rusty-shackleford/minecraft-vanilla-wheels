# D-0005 — Towing: the server follows its own tower; the axle chases the hitch; animals are passengers

**Decision**: A hitched trailer is moved by whoever moves its tower, in the tower's own
tick, right after the tower's move: the driver's client for a driven car, the server for
a scripted or driverless one -- and the server always, from its own copy of the tower.
No packet carries the trailer's position from a client; the trailer's
`isControlledByLocalInstance` is its tower's, so the driver's client ignores the server's
position updates for it the way it does for the car. The kinematics are `domain.Tow`: the
heading is the line from the axle to the hitch, held within a right angle of the tower's
heading, and the axle moves along it to the tongue's length behind the hitch; the body is
then moved through the world with `move`, so it climbs and falls. Hitching is automatic
within half a block while moving; a crouching click on the tongue, a tower gone for good,
or a stretch of a block lets go; links are saved by UUID and re-found by the server.
Animals are ordinary passengers standing on the profile's cargo slots, admitted by
`canAddPassenger` only through open doors and while `domain.Cargo` has room; a lead is
the loading tool, and a door click unloads.

**Why**: The plan had a `TowMovePayload` validated against the server's tower, but the
server has that tower already and the follow rule is deterministic, so the server can
compute the trailer itself and validate nothing; the driver's client computes the same
rule from its own tower, and because the rule is contractive the two stay within the
car's own sync tolerance. That removes a payload, a validation, and every way a client
could put a trailer where it likes. The axle rule is a real trailer's -- an axle cannot
slide sideways -- which is why it needs no damping term to tune. Animals as passengers
reuse the game's riding, saving, chunk-loading and rendering of a rider for free, and the
doors gate them through the one method the game asks before anyone boards.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A client-authoritative trailer* -- above. *A sprung or damped
heading* -- a knob for a behaviour the geometry already gives. *Animals stored as data
inside the trailer* -- a second inventory of living things to save, load and draw.

**Links**: `domain/Tow`, `domain/Cargo`, `Vehicle.follow`, `Vehicle.catchTrailer`,
`Vehicle.canAddPassenger`, `Vehicle.load`, `Vehicle.unload`.
