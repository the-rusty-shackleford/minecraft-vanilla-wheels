# D-0007 — What the playtest against Automobility settled: walls, the re-run, the boost, the riders

**Decision**: (1) A vehicle's move meets only blocks and other vehicles (never its own
trailer or tower) as walls; the living are run over. (2) A copy not at the wheel keeps
its `Drive` on the synced heading, speed, steer and drift, and re-seeds it the tick
control begins. (3) On the server, a move reported by the driver's client
(`MoverType.PLAYER`) starts with the copy stood back on the ground when it stands on
any. (4) The collision box is as tall as the hull, not the cage; riders take no
`in_wall` damage. (5) A drift's boost is a timed surge: for the charge's share of
`BOOST_TICKS` the top speed is raised by the charge's share of `drift_boost`, the speed
climbs to it and the throttle cannot cancel it; on release the wheels take the stick's
angle at once; every client is told the burn and draws the afterburner. (6) Riders' seats
turn with the body's pitch and roll, riders are drawn turned by them about the seat, and
riders turn with the body's yaw as a boat's do; the third-person camera stands back by
the vehicle's length. (7) The tyres are a loop swelling with the slide, not a screech
every twelfth tick.

**Why**: The Trailblazer was driven over one scripted course beside an Automobility
motorcar (`TrailblazerPlaytest`, in the Trailblazer's gametest source set) and the
server's log for the real box was read. The box's log held 837 "moved wrongly" resets in
a day: on a ramp of half steps at speed, the server's copy fell 0.16..0.38 blocks short
of the client's every tick, because the client presses its copy down (`vy = -0.04`)
before every move and so may always step, while the reported rise leaves the server's
copy airborne by `Entity.move`'s own rule (`onGround = verticalCollision && dy < 0`),
and the millionth vanilla shaves off the reported rise sinks it into the next block's
top -- a wall to an entity that cannot step. The re-run also disagreed wherever mobs
stood, since the boat's `canCollideWith` made every pushable entity a wall and the two
sides never agree where a cow is. Boarding turned the truck south: the client's copy was
born with heading 0 and nothing re-seeded it, and a client that spawns and boards in one
tick never has a non-controlled tick to do so. The old boost was cancelled by the
throttle the tick after release (`min(maxSpeed, v + acceleration)`), so it lasted one
tick where Automobility's lasts a second; the wheels stayed at the drift's lock for ten
ticks after release, swinging the nose a further fifteen degrees, where a released kart
goes where it points. The rider was drawn bolted upright on a nose-up truck, and the
driver's view sat still in the world while the truck spun under it to the clamp.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Our own move packet with the input, the server stepping the
model itself* -- the game's vehicle packet and its validation are what the fix leaves in
place; two channels for one move invite a second disagreement. *Automobility's mixins*
for the rider tilt -- NeoForge's living-render events do it without patching the game.
*A one-shot boost with a longer decay* -- it is the throttle's clamp that was wrong, and a
surge with its own ticks is what the reference does and what a player feels.

`Vehicle.positionRider`, `Vehicle.posed`, `client/RiderPose`, `domain/Drive` (`boostTicks`,
`boostPower`, `burn`), `client/Controls.exhaust`, `client/SkidSound`, the Trailblazer's
`TrailblazerPlaytest`.
