# D-0009 -- Fuel is the gas can

**Decision**: a vehicle is fuelled by holding right-click at it with the gas can
(`GasCanItem`), which pours two hundred ticks of fuel a tick until it is empty, the tank is
full, or the button is let go; a poured-out can is the empty can, and four coals fill it
again to a tank's worth (24 000 ticks, twenty minutes of throttle). The empty can is eight
iron ingots in a can's shape. Coal in hand fuels nothing. Part of the protocol, so every
vehicle takes it; the fuel level lives in the `fuel` data component the vehicle item already
uses. A creative hand pours from a can that never empties, and a creative driver needs no
fuel at all.

**Why**: Rusty: "instead of using coal, and only filling a little fuel at a time, can we make
... a craftable red gas can ... you can simply hold right click with a gas can to fuel
vehicles? Coal itself would no longer work for fueling." One can, one tank; the pour is the
feedback (the action bar counts up, the can's bar counts down).

**Alternatives rejected**: *one item with an empty state* -- a recipe result cannot keep the
input's item, so an empty can is its own item. *The can as a bucket of a fluid* -- no fluid,
no tank blocks; the vehicle is the tank.

**Links**: `GasCanItem`, `Vehicle.interact` (the can's click is passed on to the item's use),
`recipe/gas_can.json`, `recipe/empty_gas_can.json`, the gametests
`aGasCanPoursWhileHeldAndCoalNoLongerFuels`, `anEmptyCanCraftsFromIronAndFillsWithFourCoals`.
