# D-0010 — Lift guidance, material bevels and a restrained continuous sound mix

The lift retains its vanilla panel, slot coordinates, server decisions and button
protocol. Empty slots show original chassis/wheel/engine/dye glyphs, disappear when
filled, and describe their roles on hover. Build and Paint have explanatory tooltips;
engine-free vehicles remain supported. Engine and fuel-can icons gain controlled
upper-left bevels at native resolution without changing their silhouettes.

Engine gain eases by at most .05 per tick and pitch by .06. Its sustained gain range
is .28–.72, with .08 extra boost gain; skid gain is .20–.56. These reduce the continuous
bed by about 2 dB, keeping action sounds distinguishable, while retaining the existing
pitch range, recorded loops, spatial behavior and driving physics. Fuel now uses a
credited CC0 liquid pour rather than the obsolete coal-shovelling cue. The .38-second
cut fits the existing eight-tick repeat period. Final listening preference remains a
human judgment; numerical checks do not establish perceptual quality.

The shader booth reproduced old sampling errors: lamp faces and the lift's front stripe
fell below the sampled rows, and blue glass/deck pixels were treated as unrepainted body.
It now samples through 85% height, tests shaded paint by hue, compares body-blue loss
against the same scene's stock frame, and checks the repainted entity state alongside
visible red. Dashboard red remains its own predicate. Full completion is required.
The engine ramp is checked on a real client vehicle after resolving its sound through
Minecraft's sound manager. Hover fixtures deliver the game's actual mouse callback;
native cursor warping did not deliver that callback in the nested window.

Metals and Materials 1.0.1 is the preferred bundled build; the compatible 1.x range
remains unchanged. All work is held locally for review.
