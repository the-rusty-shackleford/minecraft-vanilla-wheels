# D-0012 — One Vanilla Wheels Creative tab groups every vehicle

Rusty requested that item-adding mods have their own Creative inventory pages. Given
separate Trailblazer, Trailer and Farmer's Pickup pages as an option, they explicitly
chose: "Put all vehicles in Vanilla Wheels".

Register one Vanilla Wheels tab: every loaded profile as a finished vehicle, then
its chassis, followed by shared lift/tools/parts/fuel cans. Read profile IDs from the
current registry when the game builds tab contents, in a stable order. Do not include
unconfigured vehicle or chassis items. Preserve the ordinary vanilla categories and
Creative search entries. Use the existing wheel item as the tab icon.

This keeps D-0001's data-only vehicle packs intact: no new code, tab descriptors or
registration is needed in a vehicle mod. Separate pages for each vehicle were rejected
in favor of finding all vehicles and assembly supplies together. Gameplay and survival
crafting do not change. Publication still requires an explicit release go.
