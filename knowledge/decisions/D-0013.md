# D-0013 — Require separately installed Metals and Materials

Rusty approved removing bundled materials copies on 2026-09-18: "Proceed."
Metals and Materials remains a required `[1.0,2.0)` dependency on both sides,
but its jar is no longer embedded. Gradle retains it on development and test
runtime classpaths. Mod Hub supplies one explicit version to clients and server.

Pack 1.39.1 demonstrated why this matters: an older materials copy embedded in
a vehicle dependency was selected. Pack 1.39.2 pinned the correct top-level jar;
removing embedded copies also removes the source of that ambiguity.

Item IDs, recipes, common tags and the old gun-mod steel alias are unchanged.
Other bundled dependencies retain their current packaging. Vehicle add-ons must
rebuild against the new Vanilla Wheels artifact to remove their indirect copies.
This changes packaging only; it does not authorize publication or deployment.
