# D-0014 — Persistent vehicle cargo, lift repairs and a paired recovery fob

Rusty approved building this revised design locally on 2026-09-18 after choosing
unrestricted recall with increasing distance cost, inclusion of hitched trailers,
and half as much durability damage as the missing tank fraction. Release is held.

Breaking a vehicle produces its packed item at zero condition. Wrenching and
recall preserve cargo, condition, fuel, paint, custom name and radio disc. A wreck
can be placed on the lift but cannot power its engine. Repairs restore condition
for `ceil(missingFraction * fullCost)` material, with the approved full costs of
20 steel / 18 iron / 12 steel for Trailblazer / Pickup / Trailer. The menu exposes
repairs only while a damaged vehicle is mounted. Creative consumes no materials.

One active motor-vehicle pairing and one valid key token per owner are stored in
overworld SavedData. Binding a new vehicle or replacing a key rotates the token.
The fob resolves the actual entity or physical drop, previews its cost, then
requires a further second of holding. Cargo is never reconstructed from a backup
snapshot. Full inventories, passengers, animals and open chests refuse atomically.
Temporary chunk tickets expire and are released on completion or cancellation.

Recall's full-tank fraction is `clamp(.05 * (distance / 1000)^2, .01, 1)`.
Different dimensions pay the cap. Fuel shortfall costs half that fraction of
maximum condition; reaching zero still returns the intact packed wreck. Only the
motor vehicle pays, including its currently hitched trailer. Creative pays neither.
Breakage and wrench pickup detach the tow link; they do not pack an attached trailer.

The index distinguishes deployed, dropped and collected objects. Physical pickup
makes recall unavailable until the vehicle is placed again. Each packed incarnation
has a token, invalidated on deployment or remote recovery, so stale world copies
cannot produce another bound vehicle. Bound drops have unlimited normal lifetime
and ordinary damage protection. Items in player/container inventories are not searched.
Fresh catalog templates stay infinite in creative; a packed cargo-bearing item is
transferred even there. Admin creative cloning and edited NBT are not an ownership
security boundary. This is not a distributed crash-atomic inventory transaction.

Cargo uses up to eight pages of 54 vanilla container components: one vanilla
ItemContainerContents stops at 256 slots and legacy entity Slot bytes wrap there.
The dedicated paged component and world NBT preserve all 432 supported slots.
Legacy world items remain readable and missing condition means pristine.
Profiles add an optional repair ingredient/full_cost (default 20 steel), so old
vehicle datapacks remain valid. Protocol 5 coordinates the new synchronized data.

The approved scope supersedes prior chest-spill behavior and transient hit damage.
It does not change artwork, handling or driving tuning. The older materials
packaging decision remains in the candidate. Local testing does not authorize a
push, public jar, pack update or server restart.
