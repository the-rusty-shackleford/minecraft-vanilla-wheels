# D-0015 — Connected redstone rolling garage doors

Rusty requested chainable garage-door blocks in Vanilla Wheels and accepted the
preview: rectangular connected panels, power opens/off closes, visible top roll,
and obstruction-aware closure. This authorizes development, not release.

Coplanar edge-connected panels form a rectangle, bounded to 16 by 16. Placement
inherits the adjoining plane and rejects expansion beyond the bound. Each block
remains an individually recoverable panel. An incomplete shape is inactive and
preserves its current panel geometry, including existing open clearance.

One bottom-corner controller caches the assembly. Topology changes rebuild it;
resting doors do no recurring region scans. Motion advances one eighth of a block
per tick, interpolated by the client. The fixed housing consumes the top half-block.
Redstone on any panel opens; loss of power closes, pausing for living entities,
Vanilla Wheels vehicles (including their parts), boats and minecarts. Dropped items
and spectators do not hold it open. Unloaded assembly chunks suspend motion and
reassembly without forced chunk loads.

Rendering reuses the existing mechanic-lift metal texture with modeled slats,
tracks and a rotating roll. Collision follows the visible curtain and housing.
The root samples exposed light instead of an opaque facade block. Incomplete
panels render their preserved local shapes, avoiding newly invented headers.

Recipe: eight steel ingots around redstone yield eight panels. It joins the
existing Vanilla Wheels tab. No vehicle art, driving behavior, data contract or
network protocol change is needed. See the verification record for the real-server
and GPU-client gates and the reproduced dismantling regression.
