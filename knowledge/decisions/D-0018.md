# D-0018 — The suspension takes seven blocks of a fall

A friend of Rusty's took damage driving the Trailblazer downhill. Rusty suspected the
rider still collided with the terrain through the vehicle, and proposed turning the
rider's collision off while seated. That was not the cause. Riders have been spared the
wall's damage since 1.5.0, a seated player's own fall distance is reset every tick, and
run-over skips the vehicle's own passengers. The damage was the vehicle's fall: vanilla's
`Entity.causeFallDamage` hands a vehicle's whole fall to every passenger, and `Vehicle`
did not override it. The drive applies only gravity, so at speed a car leaves the ground
at the brow of a hill and lands blocks lower. On a slope of one block down for every two,
at the box car's top speed of 0.89 blocks a tick, the longest flight measured 5.76
blocks, and the rider lost 4 points over two landings. Turning off the rider's collision
would not have reached this path.

Rusty chose that only big drops should hurt. `Impact.SUSPENSION` is seven blocks, and
`Impact.riderFall` passes riders what is left of a fall after it. `Vehicle.causeFallDamage`
hands that on, so each rider's own three-block safe distance still applies. A fall of up
to ten blocks is harmless, and each block past ten costs a point. This covers every
living rider, trailer animals included, and every way a vehicle moves (the server's
re-run of the driver's move, a scripted drive, a tow). The vehicle's condition, farmland
trampling and the rider's collision are unchanged.

A slope of one in one taken at top speed is a cliff to the car. It cleared all sixteen
blocks of the test hill without touching it, and the rider lost 6 points. The boost's
cap of 1.3 times top speed scales the one-in-two flight by the square, to about ten
blocks, which is right at the line; this was estimated, not measured. Whether a steep
hill at full speed should hurt is left to Rusty.

Verification is two gametests in the `vanillawheels_falls` namespace on a hillside
template. A villager with no AI rides behind an armor stand at the wheel, so the server
drives: the game seats a player ahead of anyone else, and a player at the wheel leaves
the driving to its client. Run on the old code, the hill test failed with a loss of 4 and
the cliff test with a loss of 10 against 3. With the change, both pass.
