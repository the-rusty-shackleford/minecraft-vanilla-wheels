# D-0019 — What a vehicle hands back goes where a give goes

Part of Rusty's one change for every mod that looks at a player's inventory ("all things that see
your inventory also see your backpack"): the Carried protocol (`minecraft-carried`, its D-0001),
nested in this jar.

Three places handed a player something with the inventory's `placeItemBackInInventory`, which
drops at the player's feet what the inventory cannot hold: the disc ejected from the radio, the
lead returned for each animal boarded, and a repair ingredient left in the lift's repair slot when
the repair section closes. Each is now `Carried.giveOrDrop`: the inventory's stacks of the same
thing, a carried bag's, the inventory's empty slots, the bag's empty cells, and only then the
ground. With room in the inventory nothing changes.

The packed vehicle is not among them: a wrench or a recall puts it in a free inventory slot or
leaves it where it is, and a bag refuses it (Backpacks+ D-0034).

Verified by the radio's GameTest, extended: a player with every main slot full and a bag in a
pocket ejects the disc with the empty offhand (the game tries the offhand when the main hand's
item does nothing), and the disc goes into the bag with nothing dropped. Failed on 1.9.4 (the disc
was dropped). Backpacks+ 0.6.0 is on the gametest server for it. The lead and the lift go through
the same call and are not exercised with a bag.
