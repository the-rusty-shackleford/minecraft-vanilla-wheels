# D-0020 — A crowbar pries a vehicle loose; a player's blow does nothing

Rusty, 2026-09-28: "while vehicles in general should take damage, we should be able to break or
otherwise collapse them to put them in our inventory without damaging the vehicle", then, having
weighed it, "instead of a wrench that breaks down the vehicle for you, we add a crowbar item? I
think that's how automobility did it and I see why now", and that punches should do nothing.

- **The crowbar** (`vanillawheels:crowbar`, the wrench's recipe of two steel and two iron) takes
  the wrench's place. Right-clicking a vehicle with it, crouching or not, packs the vehicle into
  the hand exactly as the wrench did (`Vehicle.pickUp`): cargo, fuel, wear, paint, disc, name and
  key binding kept. It is checked first in `interactAt`, before chests and doors, because a crowbar
  means nothing else at a vehicle. Automobility's plain right-click is the model.
- **The wrench's id is aliased** to the crowbar (`DeferredRegister.addAlias`), so every wrench in
  a world loads as a crowbar. Its icon now means condition (D-0021), and a tool drawn as a wrench
  that takes a vehicle apart would say two things.
- **The item tag `vanillawheels:pries_vehicles`** holds the crowbar and, optionally, Automobility's
  `automobility:crowbar`, which the pack carries: whichever crowbar a player picks up works.
- **A player's own blow does nothing.** `hurt` refuses a direct hit whose attacker is a player
  (`source.isDirect()`), in creative too: no wear, no break, no wreck by accident. Mobs, a
  player's arrows and bullets, explosions, fire and crashes still wear a vehicle down to a wreck,
  as D-0014 has it.

**Alternatives rejected.** Punches packing the vehicle intact (a boat's transient knock counter):
Rusty chose a tool, so a stray swing never picks up someone's car. A crouch-punch: a second
gesture that does the crowbar's job.

The sound keeps its id `wrench` (the recording it was cut for) and is named `CLANK` in code: it
also plays when a vehicle is set down.
