# D-0021 — Condition as a row of wrenches

Rusty, 2026-09-28: a hurt trailer "flashes red like a living thing taking damage. That's awkward
as hell, something else should indicate damage (my preference is a bar of wrench icons that
behave similarly to the health and hunger bars)".

- **No hurt tint.** `VehicleRenderer` draws with `OverlayTexture.NO_OVERLAY` always.
- **The row** (`client/WrenchBar`, logic in `domain/WrenchRow`): ten 9 x 9 wrenches, each a tenth
  of condition, in halves, rounded up so anything above a wreck shows. They fill from the right, as
  the hunger bar does. The sprites are built the game's way: a container (a black outline round the
  shape, dark inside) with a fill over it. A hurt blinks the row for twenty ticks, the outline white
  and what was lost shown pale, lit while `(ticks left / 3)` is odd, and a repair blinks it for ten.
  All of this is the game's arithmetic for hearts. At a fifth or less the row jiggles a pixel, as
  hearts do at four health.
- **Where and when** (Rusty's choice): above the hunger bar, stacked on NeoForge's
  `Gui.rightHeight`, so air bubbles and a mount's hearts keep their places. While riding, the
  vehicle's row, with the trailer it tows above it. On foot, the row of the vehicle under the
  crosshair, or of the vehicle that what is under the crosshair rides in: a cow aboard stands
  with its box through the trailer's wall, and on a loaded trailer the crosshair meets a cow
  first (the booth found it). Hidden where the game hides its survival bars (creative, spectator, a hidden HUD).
- The blink needs the condition's history on each client. Each client-side `Vehicle` keeps a
  `WrenchRow.Watch`, told the condition once a tick. It is allocated once per vehicle, and nothing
  is allocated per tick.

The sprites come from `devtools/art/build.py` (`HUD_ICONS`). The spanner was chosen by eye from
five candidates beside the vanilla hearts and drumsticks.
