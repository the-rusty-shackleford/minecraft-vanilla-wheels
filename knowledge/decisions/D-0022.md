# D-0022 — Letting go of a trailer

Rusty, 2026-09-28, from Bobandy_'s play: "when you detach a trailer from a vehicle and get in the
vehicle to drive away, it reattaches itself ... maybe its as simple as detaching the trailer also
adds a little bit of distance between it and the vehicle ... Like when you detach it, it rolls
back just a hair".

The cause: a moving tower catches any loose tongue within `CATCH` (half a block) of its ball
(D-0005). A crouching click lets go of the trailer with its tongue still lying on the ball, so
the first tick of driving off caught it again.

- **It rolls back a hair.** Letting go by hand sets the trailer rolling backward along its heading
  at `Tow.letGoSpeed(ROLL_BACK)`: the least speed from which it coasts three quarters of a block
  under the drive's own rule (drag, rolling resistance, the stop), found by searching the
  coasting the trailer will do. It is a roll, not a teleport. The wheels turn, every client sees
  it through the ordinary sync, and a wall behind stops it as any move is stopped. The synced
  speed is set, not only the server's drive, because a trailer's first free tick takes its drive
  from the synced one.
- **A release that holds until they part.** A roll can be blocked. So the trailer also remembers
  the tower it was let go from, and that tower does not catch it while the tongue stays within
  reach of the ball. The first time the tongue is at reach or beyond, the release is spent, and
  from then on it is an ordinary loose trailer: backing onto it hitches it again
  (`Tow.catches`, `Tow.stillReleased`, a hysteresis). The release is not saved: a save and a
  reload in the moment between a blocked roll and driving off forgets it.

Only the crouching click on the tongue does this. A tower removed, a chain stretched past a block
and a crowbar all unhitch without it, because the two are apart already or the trailer is gone.
