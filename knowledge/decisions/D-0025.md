# D-0025 — Punch to pack, right-click to repair

Rusty, 2026-09-29: "So it turns out that my friends hate the whole crowbar and breakdown system.
Can we instead make it work more like Immersive Aircraft (the mod) where if you regular punch it
with your fist, it breaks down but right clicking contextually repairs it or enters it? Investigate
the code to see how it works, don't just go off my little blurb here." Then, on the preview:
"Hunger cost should probably scale with vehicle size/class but otherwise sounds good. 1. knocks
2. hunger only 3. Paired to key fob".

## What Immersive Aircraft does (its 1.21.1 branch, read, not played)

`VehicleEntity.hurt`, `interact` and `InventoryVehicleEntity.interact`, with its config defaults:
health is one number, saved with the aircraft in the world but not in its item, so a placed
aircraft is always whole. A player's direct hit on an empty aircraft counts at least 5 against
`damagePerHealthPoint` 30, about six punches; at zero it drops as its item with its cargo spilled
beside it and never explodes from punches (crashes do). Creative: one punch deletes it. Riders'
own punches are ignored. A right-click on a damaged aircraft by anyone not aboard repairs
`repairSpeed` 0.025 a click for `repairExhaustion` 0.5, no materials, with a coloured "N%
repaired!"; crouching is not needed (`requireShiftForRepair` false), so a damaged aircraft cannot
be boarded or opened until clicked whole. Whole, the click boards; crouching, it opens the
inventory. Punching one down and placing it again is a free full repair.

## What Vanilla Wheels does

- **Punches are knocks, not wear** (Rusty's "1. knocks"). Vanilla Wheels keeps condition in the
  packed item (D-0014), so punches as real damage would pack every car as a wreck: the very
  breakdown the friends hated. `domain/Knocks`: six punches in a row (Immersive Aircraft's count),
  each within `GAP` = 20 ticks of the last, pack the vehicle into the puncher's inventory as it is
  (`Vehicle.pickUp`), a packed drop when the inventory is full. A stray swing fades. Any item in
  hand counts, a sword's hit as a fist's. Creative packs with one punch. `hurt` tells a player's
  direct hit (`source.isDirect()`) from every other source, which still wears the vehicle.
- **Who may pack it.** Nobody while anyone rides it. A vehicle paired to a key, or hitched behind
  one that is (up the tow chain), packs only for the key's owner, or a creative player (cleanup);
  the refused punch says why and counts nothing. An unpaired vehicle packs for anyone, as the
  crowbar allowed.
- **A blow rocks it** as a blow rocks a boat: `hurtTime`, `hurtDir` and a `damage` weight that
  each knock adds to and each tick takes from, drawn as a roll in `VehicleRenderer` (nothing
  counted `hurtTime` down before; it sat at ten after the first hit).
- **Right-click repairs, then boards.** The click that would seat a player (the end of
  `Vehicle.interact`) repairs a damaged vehicle a step instead (`domain/HandRepair`: `STEP` 2.5%,
  forty clicks from a wreck), with a coloured "N% repaired" as Immersive Aircraft's, a clank and a
  few sparkles; whole, the same click boards. Unlike Immersive Aircraft, the other gestures stay
  (chests, doors, radio, gas can, hitch, key, lead), so a wreck can still be unloaded and fuelled.
- **Hunger only, scaled by the job** (Rusty's "2. hunger only" and "scale with vehicle
  size/class"). The size of a vehicle's repair job is already in its profile: the lift's
  `repair.full_cost`. A full rebuild costs that many points of exhaustion (`HandRepair.exhaustion`
  = `full_cost / 40` a click), a food point per four: the Trailblazer (20) pays Immersive
  Aircraft's 0.5 a click, five food points; the Pickup (18) 4.5; the Trailer (12) 3. An empty
  hunger bar is too hungry to work (Immersive Aircraft lets a starving player repair for free).
  Creative repairs whole in one click and costs nothing.
- **The lift keeps its repair**, whole at once for material: Rusty wanted field repairs that do
  not preclude it.
- **The crowbar and the toolbox go** (D-0020, D-0023): the crowbar item, its recipe, the
  `pries_vehicles` tag, every vehicle's own crowbar and its toolbox menu. None ever shipped
  (1.10.0 is their only version). The 1.9.5 wrench's id, aliased to the crowbar in 1.10.0, is
  aliased to `minecraft:iron_ingot`, so a wrench still in someone's inventory is not lost outright.
  `OwnCrowbars`' going-home guard is kept, turned into the key's (D-0026).

## Rejected

- **Immersive Aircraft exactly**: punches as real damage and the item back at full condition.
  Picking up would repair for free, recall's shortfall wear and the lift would mean nothing.
- **Repair before every gesture**, as Immersive Aircraft gates its inventory: a wreck's cargo
  could not be unloaded, nor its tank filled, until clicked whole.
- **Steel as well as hunger**: Rusty chose hunger only. The shelved D-0024 field patches (a
  crouching crowbar, steel from a carried bag, a lowered ceiling only a lift restores) went with
  the crowbar; its work sits in the repo's stash, never committed.

## Verified

JUnit `KnocksTest` (6) and `HandRepairTest` (3). Gametests `PunchAndRepairGameTests` (5): six in a
row pack at the condition and with the cargo it had, a pause starts a new row; a rider stops it,
creative packs with one; a stranger cannot pack a paired car nor the trailer behind it, its owner
can, a creative player can; a click repairs a step for 0.5 exhaustion on a twenty-ingot job, a
wreck drives after one, an empty hunger bar refuses, a gas can's click stays the can's; creative
repairs whole and tires nobody. The older suites now punch where they pried. The booth photographs
the repair message, the rock, and the car packed by the sixth punch.
