# D-0029 — Cargo is a registry of rules; animals are its first; an empty hand loads what a player leads

Serfdom's phase 2a (its D-0003) puts captive villagers in the trailer. Until 1.10.1 only an
`Animal` boarded as cargo: `canAddPassenger`, the seat count, the cargo count, where a passenger
stands, loading, unloading and the client's rider pose each asked `instanceof Animal`.

**Decision**: `api/CargoRules` holds the rules for what rides in a vehicle's cargo. A rule says
which entities, aboard, stand in the cargo (asked on both sides), which of them a player may load
now, which item leads them, and what becomes of the tether as they board. Animals on a vanilla
lead are the first rule, built in, and behave exactly as before. Every check that asked
`instanceof Animal` asks the registry; `cargoAboard()` is everything in the cargo, and `animals()`
stays for the mods that read it.

A click on a vehicle with cargo loads with a lead item of any rule in hand, as before, **or with
an empty hand when the player leads anything a rule lets them load now**. A crouch at the open
doors with any rule's lead item unloads everything aboard.

**Why**: Serfdom's captive wears its chain; the chain is not in the player's hand, so a load that
needs the lead item in hand would need a second chain to load a captive the player already leads.
An empty hand that leads something is unambiguous: it is no seat (a cargo vehicle has none), and
only a damaged vehicle's hand repair also takes it, which a load now goes before. A cow led on a
lead is loaded the same way, so the gesture is one gesture. The rule asks what the entity *is* on
both sides because the client places passengers and poses them; Serfdom's chains are synced for
this.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A hook per gesture* (one for loading, one for placing, one for
unloading): four call sites for one question. *An entity-type tag for cargo*: it cannot say "a
villager in chains", which is state, nor what the tether becomes. *Clicking one passenger inside to
take it out*: the trailer's body and part boxes are pickable over its whole length, so a click
through the doors lands on the trailer, never on a passenger.
