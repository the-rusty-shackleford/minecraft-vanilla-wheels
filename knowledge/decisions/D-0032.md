# D-0032 — A key acts once a press

The booth's check that H under a held Left Control cycles the lights failed twice in four runs
(lights OFF, before OFF), with the H key read down and the key helper done. The cause: the game
clicks a key mapping on each of the keyboard's repeats as well as on its press (any action but a
release clicks), and the booth holds H for three seconds, past X's repeat delay (660 ms, then 25 a
second, on the booth's display as on a desktop). Every repeat sent the server a cycle: some sixty in
three seconds, the lights ending on whichever of their three modes the count left them, a third of
the time back on OFF. A player holding H a moment too long saw the same thing, and Rotorcraft's hook
key, held, caught and let go of its load the same way, since 1.0.0.

**Decision**: a key that acts once (the lights, getting out; Rotorcraft's hook and sprayer) goes
through `client/Keys.Press`: its clicks are drained each client tick, and one is a press only if the
key was not already down at the last look. The repeats come while it is down, so they are dropped; a
tap inside one tick (down and up between two looks) still counts. Keys that act while held (the
horn, up, down, the throttle) read `isDown` and were never affected.

**Why**: the game's own clicking is right for its keys that should repeat (dropping an item, a
hotbar scroll); a mode switch or a toggle should not.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Reading only `isDown`'s rise*: a tap shorter than a tick, down and up between two looks, would be
  lost.
- *Turning the repeat off in the booth*: the check would pass and players would still flicker.

**Verification**: the booth's check is now that H held three seconds cycles the lights exactly once
(the next mode); on the old code it would pass only when the repeats' count happened to come out a
multiple of three plus one.

**Links**: `client/Keys.Press`, `client/Controls`; Rotorcraft's `client/FlightControls`; D-0031.
