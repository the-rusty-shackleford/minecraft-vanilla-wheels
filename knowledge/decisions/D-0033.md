# D-0033 — The boarding line names the get-out key

Rusty, in the submarine playtest (2026-10-07): "It says 'press left shift to dismount' on the subs
even though its actually R to dismount. Left shift descends. This might also apply to the
helicopters". It did, since Rotorcraft 1.0.0: the game shows that line on boarding any ride, naming
the sneak key, and aboard a body with `verticalControls` (D-0031) Shift goes down and R gets you
out.

**Decision**: aboard a body with `verticalControls`, the boarding line names the get-out key
instead, in the game's own words ("Press R to Dismount", whatever R is rebound to). A client mixin
on `ClientPacketListener.handleSetEntityPassengersPacket` swaps the line as it is made, so the
narrator says the same. A car's line is untouched: Shift is a car's way out.

**Why**: the game's line is the first thing a rider reads, and a pilot who follows it goes down
instead of out.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Overwriting the line a tick after boarding*: the wrong one would flash, and the narrator would
  still read it.
- *Our own wording* ("Press R to Get Out"): a new string for every language, for no gain over the
  game's.

**Verification**: the booths read the line the game last showed over the hotbar right after
boarding: Rotorcraft's (a helicopter) and Submersibles' (a submarine) want the get-out key's, and
this repo's (a car) still wants Shift's.

**Links**: `mixin/ClientPacketListenerMixin`, `mixin/PlayerMixin`; D-0031.
