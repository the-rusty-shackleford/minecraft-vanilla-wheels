# D-0034 — Durability, and one blast is one blow

Friends found vehicles flimsy: one arrow took about six of the ten wrenches. A point of damage
cost 2000 condition (of 10000), the vanilla boat's five points to a wreck, for every car, truck,
trailer and helicopter alike; only Submersibles divided by a durability of its own (Explorer 4,
Scout 1.5, after Immersive Aircraft). Rusty, 2026-10-10, on the proposal "move `durability` from
Submersibles into Vanilla Wheels' profile so every vehicle declares one, cars and trucks 8, Huey
10, Chinook 12, subs unchanged": he asked first what the guns do, and on the table below, "Sure".

Measured from the code, the box's own configs read 2026-10-10 (RWM's rocket power 3, Armed
Pillagers' damage multiplier 1, which arms pillagers with RWM's pistol, shotgun, rifle, scoped rifle
and machine gun; Another Gun Mod not installed). A bullet reaches a vehicle as a blow whose direct
entity is the bullet, so it is wear, not a player's knock, and a vehicle has no hurt cooldown: every
round and pellet counts. In 1.13.0 every one of RWM's guns wrecked any car, truck or helicopter
with one hit (a shotgun's shell, two of its six pellets).

| Weapon (damage) | Car, truck, trailer (8) | Huey (10) | Chinook (12) | Explorer (4) | Scout (1.5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pistol, 10 blocks or less (6) | 7 | 9 | 10 | 4 | 2 |
| Revolver, 14 or less (10) | 4 | 5 | 6 | 2 | 1 |
| Rifle (12) | 4 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 1 |
| Scoped rifle (16) | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Machine gun (6) | 7 | 9 | 10 | 4 | 2 |

**Found on the way: a blast wore a vehicle up to five times.** A vehicle's hit boxes are part
entities (`Vehicle.Part`, four of them, the unused ones at the body's origin), and NeoForge's
`Level.getEntities` returns every part in the area: an explosion hurt the body and each part, each
passing it to the body, so a rocket or a creeper wrecked anything, whatever its durability. A bolt
of lightning is worse: it strikes everything near it on every tick it lives, through each flash,
and the body and every part each time. A living thing of many parts (the dragon) takes such a blow
once through its hurt cooldown; a vehicle has none.

**Decision**:
- A profile names `durability` (0.1 to 100, absent 1, so another mod's profile behaves as before).
  Every blow but a player's own punch, which stays a knock (D-0025), costs its amount divided by it
  (`domain/Blows.wear`). `Vehicle.durability()` is a hook: a protocol may scale it (a submarine's
  Hull Reinforcement doubles it).
- Crashes are not divided: they are charged by their protocol's `Crash` (D-0031), and a Huey flown
  into a hill at wreck speed still wrecks. Submersibles goes on dividing its own crashes, as it
  always has.
- An explosion is taken once, at the most any piece of the vehicle took (`Blows.Area`, by game
  tick: a second blast on the same tick charges only its excess, as a living thing's would).
- A bolt is taken once (`Vehicle.thunderHit`, by the bolt; a part passes its strike to the body).
- Vanilla Wheels' creative tab lists the ground vehicles only: a profile a protocol claims
  (`VehicleKinds.claimed`) is listed in that protocol's own tab (Rusty, 2026-10-10: a pane for
  every mod with recipes). `VehicleKinds.Kind`'s claim takes a `HolderLookup.Provider`, which a
  tab's builder has; its erased signature is unchanged.

**Why**: the number belongs to the vehicle, so it is the profile's, not each protocol's; the
blast and the bolt were faults that made any number meaningless for them.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Each protocol divides on its own, as Submersibles did*: a car or a trailer has no protocol but
  this one, and a third protocol would copy it again.
- *Dividing crashes too*: it would make an aircraft at durability 10 shrug off a crash at wreck
  speed; the crash speeds are each protocol's tuning.
- *A blast counts the body only*: the body's position is its middle, and a rocket on a Chinook's
  tail, eight blocks from it, would do nothing.
- *A hurt cooldown*: every pellet and every round of a burst must count, as RWM's bullet damage
  bypasses a living thing's cooldown for that reason.

**Verification**: `BlowsTest` (JUnit); `DurabilityGameTests` (a mob's and an arrow's blow divided,
a player's knock unworn, a profile with none at 1; the game's own explosion beside the box truck,
taken once at its largest piece where all of them would wreck it, and a second blast a tick later
taken again; a bolt striking every piece on three ticks taken once, a second bolt again; the tab).
Each was run against its mutation and failed: no division (three tests), no blast rule, no bolt
rule, no tab filter.
