# D-0036 — The Hidden Door

Rusty's tunnel door had to be a rock face that opens for a car. A Create contraption sinking the face
flickered under his shaders at every start and stop, even with Iris Flywheel Compat ("still looks
shitty ... lets use vanilla pistons and such for this so the animation doesn't flip flop"); of the two
ways offered, "option 2": a Vanilla Wheels door drawn by its own renderer, as the rolling garage door
is, which never swaps blocks.

**Decision**:
- The **Hidden Door** is the rolling garage door's assembly (panels side by side and upward into a
  rectangle up to 16 by 16, powered by any section) with a second curtain kind, `Shutter.Kind.SINKING`:
  the curtain is a block thick and slides down whole into its sill, leaving the full opening; no
  housing, header or tracks. Shut, every cell is a whole block.
- It sinks half a block a tick (`Kind.step` 4 eighths; the rolling door keeps 1): a four-high door
  opens in 0.4 s, so a car at full speed seen 20 blocks out never slows (Rusty: "if I am driving
  towards the door at full speed, I dont have to slow down").
- Each panel wears a solid block given to it (right-click with one; a whole, solid block without a
  block entity), drawn by the renderer as that block, lit by its own cell; none of the block is used.
  Sneaking places the block instead.
- Closing pauses while anything is in the opening, as the garage door's does; a sinking door has no
  header, so its opening is its whole height.
- Recipe: eight rolling garage door panels around a sticky piston, eight hidden door panels.
- **Found on the way, for both doors:** a panel loaded from disk scheduled its re-forming tick for the
  garage door block, so a Hidden Door's tick was dropped and the door never answered redstone again
  after a restart. The tick is scheduled for the panel's own block.

**Why**: drawn every frame from one root and never replaced by other blocks, it has no seam at the
start or end of a move under shaders; the assembly, power and obstruction rules are the garage door's,
already proven.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *A Create contraption* (built first): the seam at its start and stop under Iris.
- *Vanilla pistons*: a four-high face needs a piston per cell and a timing circuit, and still shows the
  piston heads.

**Verification**: `ShutterTest` (travel by kind, each kind's step, never past open or shut, how much of
each row a sinking curtain fills) and `HiddenDoorGameTests` (built with the real item; sinks a half
block in its first tick and is open eight ticks later, the top row cut in half on the way and the sill
row whole; nothing left in any cell when open; shuts again; waits for a vehicle in the doorway; wears a
block given to it, keeps it through saving, refuses glass; a truck and a car driven through open doors
of three sizes; a door of each kind read back from its saved panels re-forms and opens). The load fault
showed in the lab after a server restart, and the reload test failed on the old code.
