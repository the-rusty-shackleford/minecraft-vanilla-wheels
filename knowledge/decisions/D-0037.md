# D-0037 — The Floor Hatch

Rusty's elevator (a 3 by 3 Create car on a mechanical piston) leaves a hole in his house's floor when
it is down: "a floor of some kind that closes when the elevator passes underneath, and opens right
before the elevator comes back up", and "bonus points if said door also flips up a guard so nobody in
the house accidentally falls down a chasm that suddenly appeared".

Two facts shaped it, both found in his lab. A Create car moving into a block it meets stops, takes the
block into its glue and carries it on: a stone left in the shaft rode up on the car. So a cover must
never be in the car's way; it must be gone, not merely open. And the car's top at home fills the very
cells a flush cover would fill.

**Decision**:
- The **Floor Hatch** is a housing laid in a floor beside an opening, facing it; housings side by side
  are one hatch (the first, counterclockwise from its facing, drives the row so it moves as one). Each
  lays a plate over the opening in front of it, three blocks deep by default (an empty-hand click steps
  1 to 16, sneaking down): real, whole, solid blocks (`hatch_plate`, no item, cannot be mined, gone with
  their housing) that the housing takes away when it opens, so the open opening is air.
- The plate and the housing are drawn by the housing's renderer as the block the housing wears
  (right-click with one, as the Hidden Door's panels): the plate slides back under the housing in 0.3 s
  for a three-deep opening, and a hair thinner and narrower while moving so it never shows through the
  floor it slides under.
- It is wanted open while powered, while something not alive is rising toward it from below (16 blocks
  down, its top seen higher than at the last look), and while something fills the opening (the car at
  home). It waits for what rose until it arrives, is seen going back down, or is unseen for ten
  seconds. It needs no wiring for an elevator: it sees the car.
- It never opens under a foot: while someone stands on the plate it waits, unless what rises is within
  1.5 blocks under the plate, when it opens so that the one standing drops a step onto the car rather
  than the car taking the plate. It shuts only over an empty opening with nothing in the plate's layer,
  so it shuts once the car has gone below it.
- **The guard**: iron bars rise out of the housings (0.2 s) along the open edge before the plate draws
  back, stand while the hole is open and empty, and fold away once the plate is laid or the opening
  is filled. They are solid, a block high.
- Recipe: two hidden door panels and iron bars, two housings (`HBH`).

**Why**: the only cover a Create car never meets is one that is not there; laying and taking away real
blocks gives a flush floor that is walked on like any other and a true hole for the car. Seeing what
rises keeps it to one block family and no circuits, and works for any lift, Create's or another.

**Alternatives rejected** (offered to Rusty earlier, 2026-10-11): *a sunken landing with a cover sliding
under the floor a block down*, *a one-block dip while the car is away*, *a cover sliding in through the
nook's north wall from a planter outside*: each moved his floor or his house's outside, and a Create
cover would have flickered at its start and stop under his shaders as the tunnel door had. *Waiting with
the car held under a shut plate*: the car takes the plate with it. *A redstone interlock (the lever logic
in comparators, a clutch on the drive)*: more parts to hide for no gain once the hatch sees the car.

**Verification**: `HatchTest` (travel and steps, the depth's round, each reason to open, never under a
foot unless close, shutting only over an empty opening, the guard's wants and steps, what a top seen
below means, waiting and forgetting) and `FloorHatchGameTests` (laid over an empty opening; powered at
one end, the row opens, the guard up before any plate goes, the rail a block high on the far housing,
shut again; a cart rising through the opening finds it open wherever it reaches the plate's layer, and
it shuts after the cart has gone back down, lingering in the layer; someone standing keeps it shut
until the cart is close, and it is open before the cart arrives; a filled opening keeps it drawn back
with no guard, and a housing laid by a filled opening draws back; a broken housing takes its column's
plate, a stray plate goes; clicks step the depth and the plate is laid again; a given block is worn).
Each rule was run against its mutation and failed: the plate going before the guard, opening under a
foot, shutting with something in the layer, a filled opening not wanting it open. In Rusty's lab, on
his real elevator: sent down, the guard is up in 0.2 s and the floor is whole 1.6 s after the lever;
called up, the hole is open in 0.5 s, five seconds before the car arrives, and the guard folds as it
lands.
