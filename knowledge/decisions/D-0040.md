# D-0040 — Docking on the Mechanic Lift, and the lift's back wall

Rusty, 2026-10-13: "I want to enhance the mechanic lift such that you can dock/park cars and other
land vehicles on it so that you can have a garage full of uniformly parked cars ... It would center
the vehicle on the lift perfectly, and also double as staging the vehicle for modifications or
maintenance by interacting with the lift." On the preview he chose the lift's rams and half-block
rise for the move ("would be cool"), and asked that the lift have "an obvious front and back (maybe
the back side has a short wall?) and the vehicle should face the same direction". His garage (the
server repo's `knowledge/operations/garage.md`) has six lifts sunk flush in the floor with their
backs to the hall's walls.

**Decision**:
- **The back wall.** A one-block bar between the two back posts, the posts' thickness
  (`Footprint.BAR_MIN..BAR_MAX`, 6/16), drawn and solid like them; the back posts run on to meet it.
  Three new cells, indices 34 to 36, **appended** after the deck and the posts so every lift saved
  before keeps its indices. A lift from before grows its wall the first time its controller ticks
  after loading, in each wall cell that is free; a cell holding anything else is left to it.
- **A vehicle left standing on the deck is taken** (`domain/Docking`): not docked, nobody at the
  wheel, neither towing nor towed, still on the ground for `REST` (10) ticks, its centre over the
  deck. The lift must be idle; the body must fit (`fits`: no wider than the deck, no longer than
  `LONGEST`, the stretch from the wall's gap to `OVERHANG` 1.5 past the front edge: the Farmer's
  Pickup at 6.44 just does, the Huey never); no other vehicle may be docked there or stand in the
  spot. Then the lift runs its ordinary job and the vehicle glides through the hold
  (`Docking.progress`, smoothstep over the 20 ticks) from where it stood to its **spot**: the deck's
  centre line, its tail `GAP` (0.25) off the wall's face (`Docking.depth`), facing the lift's front
  -- turning the short way (`Docking.turn`), a half turn for a vehicle that came in backwards.
- **Docked** is a synced flag (`DATA_DOCKED`) and, on the server, the lift's controller position
  (`Docked` in the save). While docked the side at the wheel holds it: `Drive.atRest`, no move of
  its own, the stick dead. The server keeps it on its spot and lets it go when the lift is gone or
  it is found more than `HOLD` (1 block) from the spot (a recall, a command). The **throttle lets it
  go**: the driver's client at once, the server when the drive state arrives (`onDriveState`); a
  script's on the server. Hitching a docked trailer lets it go. A released vehicle that stops on the
  deck again is taken again; there is no Release button.
- **Build** stands a new vehicle on its spot, docked, with no glide.
- **Spot, not centre.** Rusty asked for the vehicle centred; with a wall on the deck's back row the
  Pickup (longer than the deck) centred would stand inside it, so every vehicle backs up to the wall
  instead, which is how a garage parks and what the wall is for. A Trailblazer ends a quarter block
  behind the deck's centre; a Pickup's nose hangs about a block and a third over the front edge.
  **Superseded by D-0043** (2026-10-15): with the eight-long deck (D-0042) a body that leaves a quarter
  block at both ends sits centred; only a longer one backs up to the wall.
- **The menu's status line** names what keeps the lift from taking the vehicle on its deck
  (`LiftStatus.Dock`, hard refusals first): towing, too big, taken, blocked, a driver aboard.
- A protocol built on this one may refuse docking for its vehicles: `Vehicle.docks()`. Rotorcraft
  and Submersibles are unchanged for now; the helicopters do not fit anyway.

**Why automatic, not a button**: uniform parking only happens if it is the default; a Dock button
would be skipped. And a vehicle with a driver aboard is positioned by the driver's client, so a
server-side glide would be undone by their reports: taking only a vehicle nobody drives keeps the
glide on one side. The explicit alternative was previewed and passed over.

**Why the rams**: the job already exists, says audibly that the lift did something, and lifts the
vehicle clear while it is moved, so the slide reads as the machine's work.

**Accepted**: a vehicle spun on the deck may pass through a post for a few frames (a Pickup's
corners reach past them); better than refusing vehicles that came in backwards.

**Found after release** (2026-10-14, Rusty parking the first car on the box: "floating a wee bit"): the
drawn pose's plane fit looks a block past the tail and found the back wall's top there, so a docked
vehicle was drawn 0.19 up and pitched 0.21 rad. 1.17.1 poses a held vehicle `Suspension.LEVEL`: on a
flat deck by construction, it needs no fit. The gametest asserts the flat pose, and bit on 1.17.0 first.

**Verification**: `DockingTest` (the fit by the fleet's bodies, the spot's depth, the glide's
progress, the turn, the rest watch), `FootprintTest` (37 cells, the wall last, the bars, a point in a
cell per heading), `LiftStatusTest` (the dock verdict's order), and `LiftGameTests` (left standing
it is taken to its spot facing the front, through the rise still and mid-hold between; backed in on
an east-facing lift it is spun to face the front with its tail a gap off the wall; a driver aboard
keeps it waiting; held, the stick does nothing and the throttle lets it go; a second vehicle on a
taken deck is left alone; a body too long is never taken; towing waits, a blocked spot waits, and
the truck is taken once the trailer is gone; a trailer alone docks by its own length; breaking the
lift frees it and a save keeps it docked; a lift laid without the wall grows it where its cells are
free and leaves a stone). The booth photographs the taking mid-slide and the docked car.

**Mutations** (each applied alone, the gametests run, the source restored): the rest watch never
waiting, any length fitting, the throttle never letting go, a blocked spot taken anyway, an old lift
growing no wall, and the spot moved to the deck's centre were each caught by their own test; so was a
driver aboard not holding the lift off, once the driver test let the server stand the car on the
deck first (a freshly spawned entity is not on the ground until a move, so the watch never saw it
still and the test passed for the wrong reason). The one-vehicle-per-deck guard in `take` is doubly
held: with it alone removed, `clearFor` still refuses the second vehicle, since every spot shares the
wall's gap; it stays as the plain statement of the rule and for the status line's better message.
