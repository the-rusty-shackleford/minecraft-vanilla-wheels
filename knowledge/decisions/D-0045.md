# D-0045 — Using what you hold aboard; each vehicle's own engine loop

Rusty's friends, 2026-10-22, flying the helicopters on the box: nothing held could be used in a Huey
(food, potions, the atlas), and no rotor could be heard in third person.

**The click.** From a seat the crosshair lands on the vehicle's own hull. `interact` answered a rider
as it answers anyone: with a seat free, "board", which the client takes as the click's whole meaning,
so the item in hand was never used. Every vehicle with a seat free beside its rider did this, cars
included; a full one did not.

**Decision**: a rider's click on the vehicle they ride is passed on (`hasPassenger(player)` beside
the crouch, `PASS`), and the game uses what is in their hand. What a rider's click does on purpose
stays the vehicle's, ahead of that line or in `interactAt`: a chest the click lands in, a key fob or
keyring binding, the crouching gestures.

**The loop.** `EngineSound` names its sound as it is made, from the profile, with the car's petrol
engine for a vehicle that has none. It was made as the vehicle joined the client, and a vehicle joins
before its synced data arrives, so the profile was never known then: every vehicle played the petrol
engine at the car's sixteen blocks. The Huey's rotor (`attenuation_distance` 64), the Chinook's (80)
and the submarines' motors were never heard, on the box or in a booth (booths run muted). In third
person the camera is 16 blocks back from a Huey, at the edge of sixteen.

**Decision**: `Controls.onVehicleJoined` starts the engine and tyre loops once, when the profile is
known: as the vehicle joins if it already is, else from `onSyncedDataUpdated` as the profile arrives.
The loop is kept on the vehicle (`engineLoop`, an `Object` so a server never names a client class);
`Controls.engineOf` returns it. A loop that names its own sound now plays it, at its own range.

**Verification**:
- `aRidersClickOnTheirOwnVehicleIsTheirHandsWithASeatFree` (CrewGameTests): the pilot of the box
  skids, a seat free, clicks with bread in hand and with an empty hand; the click is passed on and they
  stay at the controls. Run on 1.18.2's code first: it failed there at "the click is passed to the
  bread in hand", and only there.
- The loop, in the Huey's booth: OpenAL's own state for the playing source, read in third person on
  1.18.2's code: `vanillawheels:engine.petrol`, max distance 16, the camera 14.7 blocks off. Recorded
  through OpenAL Soft's wave-file writer (never the speakers): third person at 14% of first person's
  loudness. On 1.18.3 with the Huey's range at 200: `huey:rotor`, max 200, third person as loud as
  first. The Huey's and the Chinook's booths now check the loop playing is their own rotor at 200
  blocks.
