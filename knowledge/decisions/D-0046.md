# D-0046 — A Chinook on the lift

Found 2026-10-22, moving the Chinook's GameTests from Vanilla Wheels 1.16.0 to the current release:
the lift that builds every vehicle refused to build a Chinook at all, and has since 1.17.0, on the
box too. Three faults, each met in turn by the Chinook's own test.

1. **Build.** `occupied` asked `noCollision` of the spot's box, which counts the lift's own blocks.
   The Chinook is 3.8 wide, the deck between its posts 3.625, and 16 long, so its spot, backed to the
   wall, runs through the front posts by under a tenth of a block a side. Before the wall (1.17.0)
   the spot did not reach them.
2. **Paint and Repair.** `vehicleOnDeck` took a vehicle only with its centre over the deck. A body
   over twice the deck's length backed to the wall has its centre past the front edge: the lift lost
   the Chinook it had just built.
3. **Leaving.** Only a throttle let a docked vehicle go. Every helicopter is built docked, and a
   pilot's Space held it there; and while a player works a vehicle the server only mirrors it, so
   the server heard of no throttle and kept a climbing helicopter docked.

**Decision**:
1. `occupied` counts every block in the spot's box but this lift's own controller and parts: the
   posts it stands through are the overlap D-0040 already accepts of a vehicle spun on the deck.
   Another lift's blocks beside it, a wall or anything else still refuse it.
2. `vehicleOnDeck` takes the vehicle docked here wherever its centre is, before any other with its
   centre over the deck.
3. A protocol's hook, `leavingTheLift` (false for a car), lets go besides the throttle; Rotorcraft's
   `Aircraft` answers with the climb: the pilot's up key on their client, a script's on the server.
   While a player works a docked vehicle, the server lets it go once it is more than `HOLD` off its
   spot (`followOffTheLift`), as a held vehicle at the wheel already was.

**Verification**:
- `aBodyWiderThanTheDeckIsBuiltThroughTheLiftsOwnPostsButNotThroughAnythingElse` (a gametest profile
  `box_wide`, the long box 3.8 wide): ready to build, then refused with a stone in its spot off the
  lift, then built. On 1.18.2's code it failed at "the lift's own posts do not refuse it".
- `aDockedVehicleItsPlayersClientTakesOffTheSpotIsLetGoOnTheServer`: a player at the wheel of a car
  built docked, moved three blocks up as their client's report would move it; let go, the lift holding
  nothing. On the code before `followOffTheLift` it failed at "let go once off its spot".
- The Chinook's `theLiftBuildsIt...` builds it, paints it and flies it six blocks up off the lift on a
  scripted climb with its condition whole. Before each fix it failed in turn: OCCUPIED, NO_VEHICLE,
  "up off the lift: 0.0".

**Not seen**: a pilot's real Space off a lift. The client half is the same key reading every flight
uses (`Keys.lift`), proven on real keys in Rotorcraft's booth, but no booth flies off a lift.
